4Tasks

Private to-do lists and reminders for Android · by Mr-Bizzy, United Kingdom

4Tasks keeps your lists and reminders on your phone. Say a task to 4Dictate and it is added with its due time and reminder, and you can tick it off by voice too. Sync is optional, and goes only to a server you choose.

What it does

  • Lists, tasks, notes, subtasks, tags and priorities, with due dates, repeating tasks and reminders that arrive at the minute you set.
  • Works with 4Dictate: “remind me tomorrow at 3 to get back to Sandra about her reservation”. You confirm, then the task is made.
  • Home-screen widgets, search, filters and sorting.
  • Backs up to a file you choose and restores it. The backup never contains your sync passwords.

Private by default

There is no account with us, no advertising, no analytics and no crash reporting. Your tasks stay on your phone until you connect a sync account. Then they go straight from your phone to the server you chose, over an encrypted connection, and nowhere else. Other apps can use 4Tasks only after you allow each one by name, and none of them can delete anything.

Sync

  • CalDAV works today: Nextcloud, SOGo, Radicale, Baikal and other CalDAV servers.
  • A server with its own self-signed certificate works if you turn that on in Settings, Advanced. It is off by default, and the certificate must still match the server’s name.
  • Microsoft To Do and Google Tasks are planned.
