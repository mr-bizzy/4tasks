4Tasks User Manual

For 4Tasks 0.1.0 (beta), October 2026. Android 13 or later; works on phones, tablets and Samsung DeX.

4Tasks is a private to-do and reminder app. Your lists, tasks and reminders live on your phone. If you want them on other devices, you can sync them with a CalDAV server of your choice. There is no account with us, no advertising, no analytics and no crash reporting. It also works with 4Dictate, so you can add a task, ask what is due, or tick one off by voice.

1. Getting started

The first time you open 4Tasks it offers three buttons:

  • Add account connects a CalDAV server so your tasks sync (section 5).
  • Get started goes straight to your lists, kept on this phone only. You can add an account later.
  • Import a backup reads a backup file you made earlier (section 8).

When Android asks, allow notifications so reminders can reach you. For reminders at the exact minute, also allow Alarms & reminders (section 4).

The main screen has the app name and the open list's name at the top, ⚙ at the top right for Settings, and a bar at the bottom with the menu, search, sort and more buttons, and + to add a task.

2. Lists and the drawer

Tap the menu button at the bottom left (☰) to open the drawer. It shows:

  • My Tasks, every open task from every list.
  • Filters: Today and Recently modified. The + beside Filters makes your own filter.
  • Tags, with a + to add one. Tap a tag to see everything carrying it.
  • Your lists. Lists kept only on this phone are under Local lists; each sync account has its own heading with its lists under it. The + beside a heading adds a list there.

A list can have its own name, colour and icon. Open a list's List settings from the ⋮ menu while you are looking at it. You choose which of the drawer's filters and tags are shown in Settings, Look, Navigation drawer.

3. Tasks

Adding a task

Tap +, type the title, and tap Save (or the Done key, if you have turned that on). The task goes into the list you are looking at, or into your default list (Settings, Tasks, Task defaults) when you are in a filter. Settings, Tasks, Task defaults also sets what a new task starts with: its priority, tags, start and due dates, reminders and repeat.

The edit screen

Tap a task to open it. You can set:

The save button is at the top left and delete at the top right. The back button saves, if you have turned that on in Settings, Tasks, Edit screen options. You can rearrange or hide fields there with Customise edit screen.

Completing, sorting and finding

  • Tick the box to complete a task. A message offers Undo. Completed tasks wait under a Completed heading, which you can open or close.
  • Tasks are grouped under headings such as Due today or No due date, each in a rounded card. Tap a heading's arrow to fold it.
  • The sort button (the arrows at the bottom) sets how the list is grouped and sorted, ascending or descending: smart sort, by title, start date, due date, priority, last modified, creation time or list, or My order for a list you arrange by hand. It also chooses whether to show unstarted and completed tasks.
  • The search button finds a task by words in its title or notes.
  • The ⋮ menu has Clear completed, Collapse subtasks, Expand subtasks and Share.

4. Due dates and reminders

Add a reminder to a task with Add reminder on the edit screen. A reminder can fire when the task starts, when it is due, at a time you pick, or repeatedly after it is due. Settings, Tasks, Task defaults sets the reminders a new task gets, and Settings, Tasks, Notifications has the options for how they arrive:

  • Swipe to snooze: clear a reminder and it comes back after a time you choose (immediately, 15 or 30 minutes, 1 or 24 hours).
  • Quiet hours: no reminders between the hours you set.
  • Bundle notifications, a completion sound, and voice reminders that read the task's name aloud.
  • All day tasks: a task with a date but no time can still remind you, at a time you set.

The reminder's notification lets you complete or snooze the task.

Exact times

Android only lets an app remind you at the exact minute if you allow Alarms & reminders. 4Tasks shows a banner at the top of your list asking for it, with a button that opens the right Android screen. Until you allow it, 4Tasks still sets the reminder, but Android may deliver it a little late, sometimes by a minute or more. Allow it and reminders arrive within a second of their time.

Reminders also need notifications to be allowed. If they are not, a banner says Enable reminders.

5. Sync with a CalDAV server

CalDAV is an open standard for tasks and calendars. Nextcloud, SOGo (including mailcow), Radicale, Baikal and many hosts offer it. 4Tasks syncs with any CalDAV server you can sign in to. Microsoft To Do and Google Tasks are coming; they are not in this version.

Adding an account

  1. Settings, Accounts, Add account, then CalDAV.
  2. Type the server's URL. Often the bare address is enough (for example https://mail.example.org); 4Tasks finds the CalDAV address itself. If your provider gives a longer address, use that.
  3. Type your user and password. Tap the eye at the end of the password to check what you typed.
  4. Leave Server type on Unknown unless you know better, and tap Sign in.

Your lists appear in the drawer under the account's name. Tip: if your provider offers app passwords (mailcow, Nextcloud and others do), make one just for 4Tasks, limited to CalDAV if you can. Your main password is then never stored on the phone, and you can revoke the app password on its own.

What syncs, and when

Titles, notes, dates and times, repeats, priority, tags, reminders, completion, and the list a task is in all sync, both ways. A change you make on the phone is sent within about half a minute. 4Tasks also syncs when you open it, when you pull the list down, and in the background about once an hour. A task added by voice goes into your default list, so set a list on the server as the default (Settings, Tasks, Task defaults) if you want those tasks to sync.

Secure connections only

  • The address must be https://. A plain http:// address is refused, with a message saying so.
  • By default the server's certificate must be one your phone already trusts (a normal Let’s Encrypt certificate is).
  • If you run your own server with a self-signed certificate, turn on Settings, More, Advanced, Allow self-signed certificates. It is off by default. When you connect, you are shown the certificate's fingerprints and asked to trust that one certificate. Even then, the certificate must carry the name you connected by; a certificate for a different name is always refused. Turning the switch off again forgets every certificate you trusted.

Removing an account

Open the account (Settings, Accounts) and choose Log out. That removes the account's lists and tasks from the phone; what is on the server stays there.

6. Voice, with 4Dictate

4Dictate is our dictation app. With both installed you can speak to your tasks. In 4Dictate, start a command (its 🤖 button or key) and say, for example:

  • “Remind me tomorrow at 3 to get back to Sandra about her reservation.” Adds a task due tomorrow at 3 pm, with a reminder.
  • “What’s due tomorrow?” Shows what is due, in a notification.
  • “Mark the Sandra task done.” Completes the task whose title matches.
  • “What lists do I have?”

Short and long requests

Short requests: press the command button and speak; it stops when you pause. Longer requests, or if you think as you talk: in dictation mode, start with “4Dictate,” then say what you want, and press stop when you are done. Nothing is typed; the request is handled as a command, and changes are confirmed in the same way.

You stay in charge

  • Before 4Dictate adds or completes anything, it asks you to confirm on its own screen. Nothing changes until you press Do it. Asking what is due does not need a confirmation.
  • The answer to a question shows on 4Dictate's pad and is kept in a notification, so you can read it again.
  • If you say a name that is not an exact match but sounds like one task (“the sounder task” for “Sandra”), 4Tasks does nothing yet and 4Dictate asks “Did you mean …?” with that task. You press Do it or Cancel. If several tasks match, it tells you which; if none do, it lists some of your open tasks.
  • A reminder needs a time. A reminder time that has already passed is refused unless you asked for it.
  • Nothing can delete. There is no way for another app to delete a task or a list.

7. Apps allowed to use 4Tasks

Voice works through 4Link, a small door that lets other apps on this phone add, read and complete your tasks. It never opens by itself and nothing goes over the internet.

  • Our own apps (such as 4Dictate) are recognised by their signing certificate.
  • Any other app must ask first. A screen then shows the app's name, its certificate fingerprint, and each thing it wants to do, with what it would receive. You tick what to allow.
  • Settings, Accounts, Apps allowed to use 4Tasks lists the apps you have allowed, what each may do, and when it last did something. You can stop allowing an app there at any time.
  • The same screen shows a call log of the last 500 calls: when, which app, which function, and the result. It never records the details of a task.

8. Backup and restore

Settings, More, Backups:

  • Backup folder: where backup files go. A warning appears if it is inside the app's own storage, because Android deletes that if you uninstall 4Tasks. Pick a folder of your own.
  • Backup now writes a file straight away.
  • Automatic backups write one about once a day and keep the last seven.
  • Import backup reads a file back in. You can do the same from Import a backup when you first open the app.

Backup files never contain your sync passwords. After you restore, a sync account comes back and asks you to sign in again. 4Tasks does not use Android’s own cloud backup or phone-to-phone transfer, so a backup file is the way to move your tasks to a new phone. A synced server is another.

9. Widgets and shortcuts

Settings, Look, Widget settings has Add widget to home screen and Add shortcut to home screen. You can also add the widget from your launcher's widget list.

  • A widget shows a list on your home screen. Its buttons choose the list, open the widget's settings and add a task. Tap a task to open it, tick the box to complete it. Each widget has its own settings: which list, theme, colour, transparency, spacing, font size, and what each row shows.
  • By default a widget follows your wallpaper colours like the app. If you choose a colour for a widget, its header uses that colour.
  • A shortcut opens a chosen list straight from the home screen.

10. Settings

Tap ⚙ at the top right of the main screen. Settings has four tabs, each a column of cards, and each card opens the page it names:

TabCards
AccountsLocal lists, each account you have added, Add account, Apps allowed to use 4Tasks.
TasksTask defaults, Task list options (font size, spacing, chips, per-list sorting), Edit screen options, Date and time, Notifications.
LookLook and feel, Navigation drawer, Widget settings.
MoreBackups, Advanced, About.

Advanced has the attachment folder, the self-signed certificates switch (section 5), task-count badges on the app icon, Astrid manual sorting (an older manual sort mode, kept for anyone who uses it), and the buttons that reset your preferences or delete your task data (both ask first).

11. Colour and theme

Settings, Look, Look and feel:

  • Wallpaper colour (on by default): the app takes its colours from your wallpaper, like 4Dictate and 4Zones. Switch it off to choose a colour yourself, or to use the plain palette (navy and sky blue).
  • Theme: light, black, dark, or follow the system.
  • Markdown in titles and descriptions, Open last viewed list (or always open a list you choose), and the language (Android’s per-app language setting).

13. Privacy

  • Your tasks stay on your phone. If you connect a CalDAV account, they go straight to the server you typed in and nowhere else, over an encrypted connection.
  • No account with us, no advertising, no analytics, no crash reporting, no tracking.
  • Your sync password is stored encrypted on the phone and is left out of backup files.
  • 4Tasks does not ask for your location, contacts, camera, microphone or storage.

The full policy is at mr-biz.uk/4tasks/privacy.
