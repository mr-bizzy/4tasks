## Short description (≤80)

Private to-do lists and reminders. No account, no internet. Works with 4Dictate.

## Full description (≤4000)

4Tasks keeps your to-do lists and reminders on your phone, and nowhere else.

NO ACCOUNT, NO INTERNET
• Nothing to sign up for. The app has no internet permission at all, so it cannot send your tasks anywhere.
• No ads, no analytics, no crash reporting, no payments.

LISTS AND REMINDERS THAT WORK
• Lists, tasks, notes, subtasks, tags and priorities.
• Due dates and times, repeating tasks, and reminders that arrive at the minute you set. (Allow "Alarms & reminders" for exact timing.)
• Home-screen widgets, search, filters and sorting.
• Back up to a file you choose, and restore it.

WORKS WITH 4DICTATE
Say it, and it is a task. In 4Dictate's command mode: "remind me tomorrow at 3 to get back to Sandra about her reservation". 4Dictate asks you to confirm, then 4Tasks makes the task with its due time and reminder. Ask "what's due tomorrow?", or say "mark the Sandra task done".
• 4Tasks cannot be deleted from by other apps. There is no delete function.
• Other apps can use 4Tasks only if you allow each one, by name, and you can remove it any time.

OPEN SOURCE
4Tasks is free software (GPL-3.0), based on Tasks.org by Todoroo (Astrid) and the Tasks.org contributors. This is a separate app: it is not made or supported by the Tasks.org project. Source code: github.com/mr-bizzy/4tasks

REQUIRES Android 13 or later.
